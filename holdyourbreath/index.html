Hold Your Breath: Smoke Dash

A runner game for iPhone and Android, made by Banana Peel Games.

A giant cloud of smog is chasing Leo through New York. Hold your breath, dash through the smoke, splash cigarettes and beat the Cigar Boss.

Support

Questions, bugs or a purchase that didn’t arrive? Email support@lulucard.me. Tell us your phone model and what happened.

Restore purchases: open the Store in the game and tap “Restore purchases”. “Remove ads” and “Coin doubler” come back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it.

Privacy

The game has no accounts and does not collect your personal information. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Read the full privacy policy.
