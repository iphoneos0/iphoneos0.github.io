Privacy Policy

Hold Your Breath · Last updated November 19, 2026

Hold Your Breath (“the game”) is made by lulucard. This page explains what the game does with information about you. Short version: the game has no accounts, does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and does not sell any data. Ads are provided by Google AdMob; see “Ads” below.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, missions, upgrades, your best distance, settings) is saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android). We never see your payment details. The game only learns which items your Apple ID or Google account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device.

Ads

The game shows two kinds of ads. Reward videos only play when you tap to watch one, for example to keep running after the smog catches you or to double your coins. From your fifth run on, an ad may also show between runs, at most once every few runs; the “Remove ads” purchase turns these off. There are no banner ads.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, count views and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, how you interact with the ad, and your device’s advertising identifier. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only available if you tap “Allow” when the game asks for permission to track; you can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID any time in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices on the game’s home screen.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics

This version does not send usage data anywhere.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@lulucard.me.

隐私政策

Hold Your Breath · 2026 年 11 月 19 日更新

Hold Your Breath（下称「游戏」）由 lulucard 制作。简单说：游戏没有账号，不要你的名字、邮箱、位置、通讯录或照片，也不卖任何数据。广告由 Google AdMob 提供，见下面「广告」。

留在你手机上的

你的进度（金币、任务、升级、最远距离、设置）存在你自己的设备上，不发给我们。删掉游戏就一起删掉了。

购买

内购完全由 Apple（iPhone）或 Google Play（安卓）处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。游戏只知道你的 Apple ID 或 Google 账号买过哪几样，用来解锁和换手机时恢复。

广告

游戏里有两种广告。激励视频只有你自己点了才放，比如被大烟雾追上后看一段接着跑、看一段金币翻倍。从第五局起，两局之间也可能放一条广告，每隔几局最多一条；买了「去广告」就没有了。没有横幅广告。

广告由 Google AdMob 提供。为了放广告、计数和防作弊，Google 可能收集你的 IP 地址（能看出大概的位置）、设备和 App 的信息、你和广告的互动，以及设备的广告标识符。iPhone 上，广告标识符只有在游戏问你是否允许追踪、你点了「允许」时才拿得到，随时可以在 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 里改。安卓上，随时可以在手机设置里重置或删除广告 ID（一般在 设置 → Google → 广告，或 设置 → 隐私 → 广告）。

如果你在欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，游戏会在请求任何广告之前先征得你的同意，之后随时可以在游戏主页的 设置 → Ad privacy choices 里改。

详见 Google 隐私权政策 和 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的应用的信息。

统计

这个版本不往外发任何使用数据。

儿童

游戏不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

变更和联系

政策有变化会更新在这一页并改日期。有问题请联系 support@lulucard.me。
